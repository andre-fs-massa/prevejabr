# Project rules

- Keep post-authentication market destinations in `src/lib/authRedirect.ts`, validating same-origin UUID market paths and capturing them when a signed-out visitor opens a market, before intermediate navigation; this prevents open redirects and lost shared-market context through login, signup, or email callbacks.
- Generate `public/sitemap.xml` with `scripts/generate-sitemap.ts` before development and production builds, including public static routes and every publicly readable market while excluding auth and personal profiles; this keeps discovery aligned with available content without exposing private pages or inventing modification dates.
